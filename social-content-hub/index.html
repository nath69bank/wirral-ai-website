HOW IT WORKS

Written like a local agency would, not like a caption generator

Different for every platform

A Facebook post reads like a neighbour telling you something useful. A TikTok comes out as a script with shots and on-screen text. LinkedIn gets a point of view. The tool never pastes the same caption everywhere.

Nine tones, same facts

Direct, Educational, Casual, Humorous, Urgent, Premium, Community, Bold or Empathetic. Change the tone and the hooks, sentence length and call to action change with it. Your facts stay the same.

Nothing invented

It won't make up reviews, prices, offers or statistics. If a post needs a detail you didn't give it, you'll see a [placeholder] to fill in. Health, finance and other regulated claims get flagged against UK advertising rules.

Knows the area

Built on the Wirral, it knows Birkenhead from Heswall and New Brighton from West Kirby. Local references go in when they help your post and stay out when your audience is national.

Honest posting times

Suggested times are in UK time and labelled as starting points. Your own account insights beat any rule of thumb, so treat them as a first test.

Doesn't post for you

The free hub writes. You copy it into your own accounts. Nothing is scheduled or published without you, and it never connects to your social profiles.

Common questions

Is the Social Content Hub really free?

Yes. Your first few posts need nothing at all. After that we ask for an email address to keep it unlimited, and it stays free. Full campaigns, calendars and scheduled publishing are something we build for businesses as a separate, scoped piece of work.

Will it post to my social media accounts?

No. The free hub writes the content and you copy it into your own accounts. It doesn't connect to Facebook, Instagram or any other platform, and nothing is published without you.

Will it make up reviews or offers?

No. It only uses what you give it. Where a post needs a price, date or detail you didn't include, it leaves a [placeholder] for you to fill in. Always check the final post before publishing.

What happens to what I type in?

Your brief is sent to our AI provider to write the post and isn't used to build a profile of you. Business details you choose to save stay in your own browser. Don't paste customer names, phone numbers or other personal data into the notes.

Is it only for Wirral businesses?

No. It works for any UK business. It was built on the Wirral, so it's especially good with Wirral, Liverpool, Merseyside and Cheshire local references when they're relevant.
