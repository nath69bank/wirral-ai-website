Where the week goes

Most of the week goes on work nobody started a business to do.

Replying to the same questions. Copying details from one system into another. Chasing quotes, invoices and reminders. Trying to post something, anything, on social media. It all matters, and none of it needs you personally.

01

Enquiries that wait

A message lands at 9pm or while you are on a job. By the time you reply, they have asked someone else. An AI assistant answers straight away, day or night, and books the ones worth your time.

02

Admin that repeats itself

Quotes, confirmations, follow ups, reminders and data typed twice. If it happens the same way every week, it can usually run on its own, with you checking the bits that matter.

03

Content that never gets posted

You know you should be posting. There is never a quiet hour to do it. AI can write it in your voice and schedule it, so your socials keep going while you work.

04

Reports rebuilt by hand

The same spreadsheet, the same numbers, every month. Let the systems talk to each other and the report is simply there when you need it.

The boring tasks don't need you. Your customers, your craft and your ideas do.

We use AI to increase efficiency and take the weight off your workload. Not by replacing the people who make your business good, but by giving them back the hours that repetitive work quietly eats. You stay in control of anything that matters. The rest just gets done.

How it works

Chat, map, build, hand over, look after.

Simple on purpose. You tell us what eats your week, we take the biggest job off your plate first, and we keep going for as long as it is useful.

  1. 01

    Chat

    A free, friendly conversation about your business and where the hours go. Start it with our AI assistant any time, and it books you in with us.

  2. 02

    Map

    We list the repetitive jobs, pick the one that frees up the most time for the least fuss, and agree a fixed price before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    We set it up around the tools you already use, test it on real work, and keep a person in the loop wherever the stakes need one.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    You and your team are shown how it works in plain English, with a short written guide, so nobody is left guessing.

  5. 05

    Look after

    We keep an eye on it, tune it as your business changes, and move on to the next boring job when you are ready.

Common questions

Straight answers before you get in touch.

What kind of tasks can you automate?

Anything repetitive that follows the same steps most of the time. Answering and booking enquiries, sending quotes and confirmations, reminders and follow ups, moving details between systems, monthly reports, and writing social media posts are the most common. If you are not sure, ask the assistant and describe your week.

Will an AI assistant really talk to my customers?

Yes, by text and by voice, trained on your services, prices and the questions you get asked most. It answers straight away, asks the questions you would ask, and hands the good leads to you with all the details, so you only spend time on the conversations worth having. The one on this page is a working example.

We're not techy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of the businesses we work with aren't. We set everything up around the tools you already use, show you how it works in plain English, and look after it once it is live.

How do you handle our data?

Carefully, and as part of the design. We look at what a tool would see, where it goes, what should never be shared, and where a person needs to stay in the loop. You will know exactly what is running and why.

What does it cost?

It depends on the job, so we agree a fixed price after a short chat, before any work starts. Our Content Hub app is free to start, with paid plans from £9 a month, if you would like to see what AI can do for you first.

How does it start?

With a free conversation. Ask the AI assistant to book you in, or message us on WhatsApp. We will talk through your week, pick the first job to take off your plate, and agree a fixed price before anything begins.
