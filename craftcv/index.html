How it works

Three ways to work with an AI CV editor, not a template.

Polish

Fixes grammar, passive voice and clunky phrasing without changing what you actually did or how you say it. Good for a draft that's mostly there but reads flat.

Expand / brainstorm

Give it a rough note — "handled customer calls", "fixed bugs" — and it asks for the scope and result behind it, then offers a few bullet point variations to choose from.

Keyword match

Paste an excerpt from a job advert and CraftCV checks your draft against it, flagging relevant skills that are missing and suggesting how to work them in without sounding forced.

It's built as a CV editor rather than a CV generator: nothing lands in your draft until you click "Insert into draft" on a suggestion you actually want, so the words stay yours.

Common questions

Is CraftCV free to use?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no account and no price for using the workspace. wirral.ai built it as a live example of the AI tools it builds for clients.

Will CraftCV invent experience, numbers or achievements I didn't give it?

No. If you give it a vague line, it asks for the missing scope or result rather than guessing a figure. Any suggestion only reaches your draft if you click Insert into draft yourself.

Does CraftCV help with ATS (applicant tracking systems)?

Yes. Suggestions favour standard, machine-readable bullet structure and plain formatting, and Keyword match mode checks your draft against a job spec so the phrasing an ATS or recruiter searches for is more likely to be present.

Can I check my CV against a specific job advert?

Yes — paste an excerpt of the job spec into the panel under your draft, switch to Keyword match mode, and CraftCV will point out relevant skills missing from your draft and suggest organic ways to add them.

Is my CV data stored or shared anywhere?

Your draft and job spec are kept only in your own browser's local storage so they're still there if you come back. They're sent to the AI model to generate a reply, but there's no account, no database and nothing shared beyond that.
